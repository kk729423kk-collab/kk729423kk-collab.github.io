01 / About

Engineering complete systems across disciplines.

I am currently a professional master's student in Biomedical Engineering (BME) under a joint training program between Sun Yat-sen University and Shenzhen University of Advanced Technology (SUAT).

My undergraduate background is in Integrated Circuit Design and Integrated Systems at the University of Jinan. My interests span biomedical engineering, brain-computer interfaces, neural signal acquisition, embedded systems, FPGA-based intelligent systems, robotics, computer vision and hardware-software co-design.

I am particularly interested in building complete engineering systems from hardware acquisition and embedded control to signal processing and intelligent algorithms.

Hardware acquisition → Embedded control → Signal processing → Intelligent algorithms
